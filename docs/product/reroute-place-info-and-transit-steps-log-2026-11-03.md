# Reroute place info and transit steps log

**Status:** Implemented on 2026-11-03 on the branch `feature/place-info-and-transit-steps` for review. Not merged, not pushed and not deployed; `main` (the demo build) is unchanged. Checked in a desktop browser at phone size against live Google and Translink data; not tested on a real phone or with participants.

**Who wrote it:** requested by T.P; the plan, code, tests and this log were written by Claude Opus 5.5 through Claude Code (see the [AI-use log](../team/ai-use-log.md)). Human review is required before this is treated as final, especially the Google cost of the place sheet, the Privacy page wording and how the screens feel on a real phone.

Paths are in `apps/plan-b-mvp/`.

## What was asked

1. A Google-Maps-style information sheet for a place chosen on the map: open now, address, photos, reviews, rating, and buttons to call, share, save and open the website. It opens from the place card under the map and slides up out of it, with the same motion feel as the rest of the app. Below the buttons: a sideways photo gallery with each photo animating as it scrolls in, then an Overview (opening hours that expand to the week, a few reviews, business info and an About summary of address, phone and website). The Overview title is a menu for Reviews, Photos, Updates and About. "Use this place" sits at the top. A plain "i" beside the place in "Where are you going?" opens the same sheet over that page.
2. Live directions: fix live bus positions and early/late data; group walking turns into one "Walk 7 min" step (turns only on tap, never in notifications); dots for every stop along a bus route; the correct bus icon instead of what looked like a calendar; a multi-line bus step (boarding stop, an indented part joined by a thick rounded bar with the line pill, destination, live departure time or "Now", early/late text, other departures with "More departures", "Ride X stops (Y min)" with the stops in between, then the stop to get off at with its arrival time); the destination's name instead of "Arrive at your destination".
3. Remove the leaf animation on the CO₂ elements.

## Decisions and limits

- **No Updates view.** Google's Places API does not provide a business's updates or posts, and it has no menu link, so the menu offers Overview, Reviews, Photos and About.
- **Google cost (assumption, check current pricing):** a plain tap on the map is still one Place Details Pro request. Opening the sheet adds one Place Details Enterprise + Atmosphere request (the costliest Place Details SKU, because of reviews and the summary), cached for 15 minutes, and each photo that scrolls into view is one Place Photos request (cached 10 minutes). Both count towards the daily address-lookup cap; photos also have their own hourly limit per visitor (`PLANB_PHOTO_LIMIT_PER_HOUR`, default 120). Google allows at most 5 reviews and 10 photos per place.
- **Departures** come from the static timetable plus live times; a service that is not in the realtime feed shows "Scheduled". "Other departures" uses the exact boarding stop and any stop within 150 m of the stop off.
- **Stop times in the "Ride N stops" list** are the timetable's, moved by the boarding delay; the arrival time prefers the live prediction at the stop off.
- **Walking-only routes** are also one walking step; the turns are still there on tap.
- **Matching a step to its timetable trip** without a realtime trip id picks the trip on the same line leaving nearest Google's time (within 15 minutes); on frequent lines with long delays it could pick the neighbouring trip.
- The stop dots are on the live-directions map only, not the route preview maps.
- The timetable database is about 172 MB in the data directory on the server.
