## Decisions and deviations

- **Place icon:** Google's glyphs are one-colour SVG masks (black in the files checked). The app draws them white with a CSS filter on the place's colour, as Google Maps does. The Terms are unchanged; the Privacy Policy and About / Data Sources now say that tapping a place loads its icon from `maps.gstatic.com` and that Place Details returns the icon and colour.
- **Next-hour rain:** The request said "if available". Predicted radar images are not available from RainViewer's free API any more, so the app shows Open-Meteo's model forecast. It is labelled as a forecast, not radar, everywhere it appears, and the radar map itself only ever shows real radar.
- **Time label:** "Latest" and "Forecast" now sit above the clock time on the radar bar, so the slider keeps its width on a 360 px screen.
- **Service worker:** `VERSION` in `sw.js` was bumped to `planb-reroute-7` so installed copies fetch the new files.

## Known issues

- **Not seen on a real Google map:** No Google key was available, so neither the map picker nor the radar map loaded. The picker overlay, long-press, pin dragging and the radar's forecast steps were checked in headless Chrome against a small stand-in for `google.maps` with a flat projection and no map tiles (see below). That checks Reroute's own code and CSS, not Google's behaviour (for example where Google reports a tapped place, how its touch handling reacts to the pin, or how the overlay redraws while zooming). **The team must check both on a phone with the deployed browser key.**
- **Long-press and drag on phones:** Neither has been tried on iOS Safari or Android Chrome. The press timer, the pinch cancel and the pin drag were written for both.
- **Google's own place icon stays visible** under the enlarged pin (see the first row).
- **Forecast timing:** For Australia the 15-minute steps are interpolated from hourly model data, so a shower can arrive earlier or later within the hour than shown.
- **Place lookups count even when cached** (unchanged from the previous round).

## What was checked

- 283 offline unit tests pass (`python -m unittest` from `apps/plan-b-mvp/`, with the packages in `requirements.txt`). These are the earlier 275, with the Place Details field-mask test updated, plus new tests. The new tests cover the icon fields and their validation (a non-Google address, a script address, a bad colour and an address that already has an extension are dropped), a place without icon fields, the next hour's four 15-minute steps after "now", a dry hour, missing values and a missing chance of rain, the hourly fallback, the request asking for `minutely_15`, the simulated next hour, and `GET /api/weather?detail=1` returning `next_hour` in demo mode. They make no network calls and none exercises the front end. `python -m py_compile` passes on the Python files and `node --check app.js` passes.
- A live request through the local server on 2026-10-26 returned four 15-minute steps for Brisbane from Open-Meteo.
- **Weather layout:** a script drove the app in headless Chrome at 360, 390 and 430 px widths in dark mode, with demo storm weather and live weather. It measured every element in the stat cards and the next-hour card: none extends outside its card, and the page has no sideways scroll. The only element wider than its box is the deliberately clipped list for screen readers. The same script on the `main` code reproduced the bug: the wind arrow stuck out of the Wind card by 26, 16 and 3 px (storm) and 11 and 1 px (live) at 360, 390 and 430 px.
- **Destination:** with a last destination and a Work/Uni address saved, Plan a trip opened with To empty, and the Home and Work/Uni chips and "Choose on map" were shown. On `main` the same setup filled To with the last destination. With demo mode's preset trip, both fields were filled as before.
- **Map picker and radar, against a stand-in for Google Maps:**
  - A long press dropped the pin with its tip on the pressed point (0 px across, 0.01 px down).
  - Dragging turned it into a circle (`border-radius: 50%`) with map gestures off. Letting go restored the gestures, bounced it back into the pin shape and moved the chosen position by exactly the dragged distance.
  - A long press did not fire on the pin itself.
  - A tapped place with a fake Place Details answer popped up in the neutral colour, then took `#FF9E67` with the Google cafe glyph in white.
  - A failed lookup turned into a dropped pin with its note.
  - With reduced motion on, the drop animation lasted 1 ms.
  - "Use this place" filled To and removed the overlay.
  - With RainViewer's real frame list (13 frames) and the live forecast (4 steps), the slider ran from 0 to 16 and opened at "Latest". A forecast step kept the latest radar frame, showed the tag and highlighted its bar. Play visited all 17 steps.
- A reviewing subagent read the whole change for bugs. It found no crash-level problems and eight smaller issues. Fixed:
  - the next-hour card not refreshing when the forecast arrived after the radar frames;
  - the black glyph and its tilted fade-in;
  - a finished place lookup or a second finger moving the pin during a drag;
  - a stale slider range during playback;
  - old forecast steps;
  - unchecked numbers.
  - Kept by choice: the place pin stays at the tapped point.
