# Design System: Reroute

Recorded on 2026-10-27 from the shipped code in `apps/plan-b-mvp/` (`app.css`, `index.html`, `app.js`, `favicon.svg`, `icons/`). The original intent is in [`docs/design/design-system.md`](docs/design/design-system.md). Where the code and that brief disagree, this file records what the code does. The differences are listed in the appendix at the end. The fictional interview scenario (`demo.html`, `demo.js`, `styles.css`) is not part of this system.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Dispatcher"**

Reroute looks like a calm control room at dusk. Near-black surfaces are stacked in quiet steps, and the content sits on them in a single column. One indigo signal says where to act. Colour that means trouble is held back until something has actually gone wrong. A traveller who opens it on a platform should see the time, the trade-off and the next action before they notice the interface.

The density is phone-first and generous. There is one 480 px column, a 24 px gutter, 32 px between sections and 16 px between cards. Hierarchy comes from weight and size contrast in a single family (Inter), not from colour. Headlines are tight and slightly negative-tracked. Small uppercase labels are tracked wide. Every number is tabular. Status (on time, late, cancelled, CO2 saved) is carried by soft tinted chips and lines. The tints are mixed from one tone rather than drawn from a large palette.

Dark is the default and the reference theme. A derived Light theme exists, and Auto follows the system setting. Motion is tactile and calm. Presses squeeze slightly, pages push sideways, sheets rise with a soft ease-out, and switches settle on a small spring. Everything collapses to near-instant under reduced motion.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark-first layered surfaces (void, base, surface, card, pop) with 1 px translucent borders.
- One action colour, Platform Indigo. Green, amber and red are kept for status.
- Inter at 400 to 700, tight negative tracking on headings, and tabular numbers everywhere.
- Generous rounded corners that step down as elements nest (28 px sheets down to 6 px tags).
- Tactile, calm motion: 0.98 press squeeze, glow focus rings, and shimmer skeletons instead of spinners.
- Mode colours (bus, rail, ferry and others) used only for route segments and mode tiles.

## Colors

A cool near-black ladder with one indigo signal, three status hues and a set of mode colours. All are defined as CSS custom properties on `:root`. `:root[data-theme="light"]` swaps them for Light.

### Primary
- **Platform Indigo** (`accent`; `light-accent` in Light): primary buttons, the Plan a trip button, selected chips and segmented thumbs, switch tracks when on, the goal ring, the current chart bar, the demo pill and the rerouted line in the hero art. Its lighter text variant (`accent-text`) is used for links, text actions and the "Re" of the wordmark. Its soft and glow variants (`accent-soft`, `accent-glow`) are used for selected backgrounds and focus halos.

### Neutral
- **Void** (`bg-void`): the page behind the app column on desktop.
- **Night Base** (`bg-base`): the screen background, Settings and the side menu.
- **Raised Surface** (`bg-surface`): cards, grouped lists, sheets, the hero and tiles.
- **Card Inset** (`bg-card`): fields, secondary buttons, inset boxes inside cards, and grouped lists inside sheets.
- **Pop** (`bg-pop`): anything floating above the page, such as suggestion lists, toasts, the profile menu, map controls and dialogs.
- **Mist Text** (`text-main`), **Slate Text** (`text-muted`) and **Faint Slate** (`text-faint`): primary text; secondary text, placeholders and small print such as the suggestion-list footer; and decorative marks (chevrons, the hero's dotted route, the avatar, empty streak weeks) and disabled steppers. Faint Slate is never used for text a traveller must read.
- **Hairlines** (`border-subtle`, `border-strong`, `divider`): resting borders, hover or floating borders, and row dividers.
- **Fills** (`fill`, `fill-strong`): hover and press washes, skeleton blocks, neutral chips and tracks.

### Status
- **Signal Green** (`success`): on time, fits all limits, CO2 saved, the Impact button, logged trips and clean streak weeks. Its text variant (`success-text`) is one step darker than the fill tone in Light (#065F46 on a #047857 tint), so text on the tinted chips stays above 4.5:1.
- **Platform Amber** (`warning`): tight buffers, late services, weather and offline banners, and stale live data. In Light its text variant (`warning-text`, #92400E) is likewise one step darker than the tint tone (#B45309).
- **Disruption Red** (`error`): cancellations, missed deadlines, the Journey disrupted entry and its Go button, and destructive confirmations.
- **Harbour Teal** (`teal`) and **Forecast Violet** (`violet`): the offline-mode banner, equivalent-distance icon and wind arrow; the next-hour rain forecast.

### Mode colours
`seg-walk`, `seg-bus`, `seg-rail`, `seg-tram`, `seg-ferry`, `seg-car`, `seg-rideshare`, `seg-scooter` and `seg-bike` colour route segments on maps, step icons and mode tiles. On the map, GTFS route colours replace them when a line is matched.

### Named Rules
**The One Signal Rule.** Platform Indigo is the only colour that means "act here". Status hues never fill a primary action. The single exception is the Journey disrupted screen, where the Go button is red because the whole screen is about disruption.

**The Tinted Glass Rule.** Status, mode and icon tiles are tinted by mixing their tone into transparency at 14 to 15% (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--tone) 14%, transparent)`), with a 22 to 30% border mix for banners. New states reuse this recipe. They do not add new hex values.

**The Words With Colour Rule.** Every coloured state also has a text label ("On time", "Lowest CO2", "Misses your deadline"). Colour alone never carries meaning.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Inter, the same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** none. Numbers use Inter's tabular figures.

**Character:** one neutral grotesque used at four weights (400, 500, 600 and 700, loaded from Google Fonts). Contrast comes from size jumps and negative tracking on large figures, not from a second family.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 48 to 64 px, line height 1): big figures, such as the Journey disrupted arrival time (52 px), the weather temperature (56 and 64 px) and the Impact Dashboard total (48 px). Tracking is -0.05 to -0.06em, with tabular figures.
- **Headline** (600, 30 to 34 px, 1.1 to 1.12): screen titles, the home hero (32 px) and the Journey disrupted heading.
- **Title** (600, 22 to 26 px, about 1.15 to 1.2): sheet titles (22 px), the results heading and next-steps confirmation (26 px), and the live-directions distance.
- **Title Small** (600, 15 to 17 px, 1.3): option-card names (16 px), tile headings (15 px) and Journey disrupted steps (17 px), tracked -0.02em.
- **Body** (400 or 500, 15 px, 1.5): rows, step text and inputs. Inputs that take typing are 16 px so iOS does not zoom.
- **Body Small** (13 px, 1.5 to 1.6): hints, trade-off sentences and secondary row text.
- **Fine** (12 px): footnotes, attribution and badges (badges at 600).
- **Label** (600, 11 px, +0.12em, uppercase): eyebrows, section headers and field labels (From, To).

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Numbers Rule.** Every time, price, distance, count and CO2 figure uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, so columns and countdowns do not jitter.

**The Tracking Follows Size Rule.** Tracking tightens as text grows (-0.01em body, -0.02em titles, -0.03em headlines, -0.04 to -0.06em display) and opens only for small uppercase labels (+0.06 to +0.14em).

## Layout

There is a single column, full screen on phones with safe-area insets. From 520 px wide it becomes a centred column up to 480 px, with side hairlines and a wide soft shadow over the void colour. Page content scrolls inside a 24 px gutter (`spacing.gutter`), with 24 px of top padding and 40 px plus the safe area at the bottom. Sections are separated by 32 px, cards stack 16 px apart, and button stacks and chip rows use 12 px and 8 px gaps.

Lists follow a grouped-row pattern. Rows are at least 56 px high with 12 by 16 px padding inside a rounded group card. Hairline dividers are inset 16 px, or 66 px when the row has an icon tile. A small uppercase header sits above each group and an optional footnote below it. A translucent blurred navigation bar (at least 56 px) and sticky footer frame each pushed screen.

Most spacing is on a 4 px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32). Several components also use 6, 10, 14 and 18 px for optical balance. Narrow-screen adjustments happen at 359, 369, 389, 399 and 400 px (max-width). Below 360 px the Home button collapses to its icon and the weather stats go to two columns. Below 390 px the home header tightens. Below 400 px datetime rows wrap and weather labels stack.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a hybrid. The main cue is tonal layering: void, base, surface, card, pop, each a step lighter in Dark and a step whiter in Light, and each separated by a 1 px translucent border. Soft ambient shadows reinforce the layers rather than defining them. In Dark they mostly read as a gentle darkening below cards. In Light the card shadow gains a 1 px contact line. Floating layers (menus, toasts, map controls, dialogs, sheets) use a deeper shadow and the strong border. Navigation bars, sticky footers and the live-directions card use 18 px backdrop blur over a translucent fill.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`--shadow-card`: Dark `0 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`; Light `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 18, 34, 0.04), 0 10px 30px rgba(15, 18, 34, 0.07)`): resting cards, tiles and group cards on the page.
- **Pop** (`--shadow-pop`: Dark `0 18px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)`; Light `0 18px 48px rgba(15, 18, 34, 0.18)`): sheets, suggestion lists, toasts, menus, map controls, dialogs and FABs.
- **Focus halo** (`0 0 0 5px var(--accent-glow)` on buttons, chips and icon buttons; `0 0 0 3px` on fields): added beside the 2 px focus outline or focus border.
- **Best-option ring** (`0 0 0 3px var(--success-soft)` over the card shadow): the top-ranked option. Your own ride post uses the same ring in `accent-glow`.
- **Trip button glow** (`0 12px 32px color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 34%, transparent)`): only the home Plan a trip button.
- **Panel edge** (`±16px 0 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`): Settings and the side menu as they slide over the page.

### Named Rules
**The Surface Ladder Rule.** A layer sits one step above what it rests on. Page cards are `bg-surface`. Anything inside a card is `bg-card`. Anything floating over the page is `bg-pop` with the pop shadow and `border-strong`. Inside a sheet, group cards drop their shadow and use `bg-card`.

## Shapes

Corners are generously rounded and step down as elements nest. Sheets are 28 px (top corners only). Hero, confirmation, KPI and dialog panels are 24 px. Option cards are 20 px and group cards 18 px. Banners, toasts and floating buttons are 16 px. Buttons and icon buttons are 14 px, fields 12 px, icon tiles 10 to 12 px, and line and simulation tags 6 px. Pills (chips, badges, live-status tags) round to half their height. Step numbers, avatars, dots and knobs are full circles. The rows inside a group card follow its corner (17 px, the 18 px card minus its border), so focus rings stay rounded.

Borders are always 1 px and translucent. The only 2 px strokes are the focus outline and the offline area box. Icons are a Lucide-style inline SVG set drawn in `app.js` on a 24 px grid, with 1.75 px strokes (2.1 px below 16 px), round caps and joins, and `currentColor`. They are drawn at 14, 16 and 20 px in text and at 24 px or more in tiles. The logo is a 64 px rounded square (15 px radius) with an indigo gradient (#6D70F5 to #4F46E5). On it, a faint dotted direct route is crossed by a pale-red disruption mark, a white route curves around it, and the start point is green.

### Named Rules
**The Nested Radius Rule.** An element inside another always has a smaller radius than its container (card 20, arrival box 14, fact pill 10). Do not put a 20 px element inside an 18 px one.

## Components

### Buttons
Tactile and calm. They are full-width by default, filled, and squeeze slightly on press.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (14 px). At least 52 px high, or 48 px in two-up rows and 44 px in compact contexts.
- **Primary:** Platform Indigo fill with white text at 15 px / 600. Hover brightens it by 8% (only where hover is available).
- **Secondary:** Card Inset fill, hairline border and main text. Hover raises it to Pop with the strong border.
- **Text:** no fill, muted text at 500, and a faint wash on hover.
- **Next steps:** Start directions is the only filled action in the stack. "I'm taking this trip" is a secondary button with a leaf icon, so green never fills an action.
- **Press / Focus / Disabled:** press scales to 0.98 at 86% opacity. Focus is a 2 px indigo outline plus a 5 px glow halo. Disabled is 45% opacity. While loading, the button becomes an indigo-tinted shimmer (red-tinted for Get me there) that says in words what is being checked, such as "Checking routes and live services…".
- **Plan a trip (signature):** an 84 px indigo panel with a 20 px radius, a 48 px translucent-white icon tile, a 19 px title with a 13 px sentence under it (full white, so it keeps 4.5:1 on indigo), and a chevron. It is the only element with a coloured glow shadow.
- **Journey disrupted entry and Go:** the home entry is a surface card tinted 9% red with a red icon tile and a slow pulsing ring. On the Journey disrupted screen, which is itself tinted 6% red, the Go button is a 64 px solid red button with an 18 px radius at 18 px / 700. The answer under it is a 24 px-radius surface panel: the 52 px arrival time with "arrival" on its baseline (wrapping below it on narrow screens), a 15 px buffer line in the buffer colours ("3 min before your 8:55 am deadline"), the reason sentence, and neutral fact pills naming each disrupted option it avoids ("Avoids the 412 · Translink alert · Simulated").

### Chips
- **Style:** pill (18 px radius), at least 36 px high for buttons and 32 px for static chips, with an invisible extension that brings the hit area to 44 px. Card Inset fill, hairline border, muted 13 px / 500 text.
- **State:** selected chips (`aria-pressed`) turn indigo-soft with indigo text and a 50% indigo border. Live chips are green-soft with a pinging dot.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 20 px for option, ride, chart and timeline cards. 18 px for group, tile and sync cards. 24 px for hero, confirmation and KPI panels.
- **Background:** Raised Surface, with Card Inset boxes inside (arrival time, maps).
- **Shadow Strategy:** the card shadow (see Elevation & Depth). The best option adds the green ring and a 38% green border.
- **Border:** 1 px `border-subtle`, becoming `border-strong` on hover.
- **Internal Padding:** 20 px (24 px on panels). Option cards end in a full-width 52 px text action above a hairline.
- **Entrance:** cards rise 12 px and fade in over 420 ms.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Card Inset fill, hairline border, 12 px radius, at least 44 to 48 px high, and 16 px text. Inside grouped rows, the From and To fields have no box of their own: the whole row gets a faint wash and an inset 1.5 px focus ring.
- **Focus:** the border turns `border-focus` with a 3 px `accent-glow` halo. There is no outline.
- **Clear button:** every text field shows a 22 px circular clear dot inside a 44 px target once it has text. It scales in from 0.8.
- **Error / Disabled:** errors are shown in banners and inline notes, not on the field itself. Disabled steppers and rows drop to 45 to 50% opacity.

### Navigation
- **Pushed screens:** a 56 px translucent bar (82% base with 18 px blur and a bottom divider). It has a muted "Back" text button on the left, and a 44 px Home button and actions on the right. Screens push in from the right over 380 ms, and the outgoing screen slides 24% left and dims to half opacity.
- **Sheets:** they rise from the bottom with a 28 px top radius, a 36 by 4 px grab line, a 22 px title, and a scrim that fades with the drag. They can be dragged down to dismiss.
- **Side menu and Settings:** full-height panels that slide in from the left (menu, up to 360 px) and from the right (Settings) on Night Base, with the panel-edge shadow. The menu's groups stagger in 40 ms apart.
- **Segmented control:** a recessed base track with a raised card thumb that slides on a spring. The selected label turns main and 600.
- **Focus between screens and layers:** the side menu, Settings, sheets, pop-ups, a full-screen map and dialogs are modal. While one is open, the screen and any layer under it are `inert` and Tab wraps inside it. Escape closes the top one, and focus returns to the control that opened it (or, if a re-render replaced that control, to its twin with the same data attributes). A new screen moves focus to its first `h1` or `h2`; screens without one lead with the Journey disrupted? answer, the directions banner or the area hint. These lead elements take focus without an outline, because they are not controls.

### Option card (signature)
This is the core decision surface. It has a 40 px mode tile tinted in the mode's colour, then the option name and a sub-line, then an inset arrival box (24 px tabular arrival time, a coloured buffer word and a stat column). Below that are a row of 10 px-radius fact pills (good, warn or bad tints), a CO2 line, a one-line trade-off sentence, and a full-width text action. The CO2 line is a tinted 14 px-radius strip with a 32 px icon tile only on options that make it (on time when a deadline is set, and not disrupted). On the others it is one muted 12 px line with a 14 px leaf, and in their detail sheet a neutral, untinted strip. The Lowest CO2 badge goes only to an option that makes it; it is outline-only (green text, 45% green 1 px border, no fill) and wraps under the strip's text rather than narrowing it. Indigo `badge info` pills mark "Price unknown" (an option that fits every other limit but has no known price) and, in results opened from Journey disrupted?, "Best way now" stacked under the status badge. The body above the action is one button that opens the detail sheet. It has no `aria-label`, so its own text (name, arrival, status, reasons) is what a screen reader announces, and it is the card's only tab stop. The text action stays clickable but is skipped by Tab, because the sheet repeats it.

### Live status tag and live dot
Pill tags (20 px radius, 12 px / 600) with a 6 px dot in the current colour read "On time" (green), "N min late" (amber, red when major), "Cancelled" (red), "early" (indigo) or "No live data" (neutral). A 7 px live dot pings outward every 2 s. It turns amber when the data is stale.

### Map
Google Maps fills an 18 px-radius, hairline-bordered frame 240 px high. It expands to full screen with a 380 ms grow. The floating control stack (44 px buttons on Pop, 14 px radius) sits top right. In Dark, the map uses a custom style: land #202431, labels #AAB2C0, roads #343A49, water #111927, points of interest hidden. In Light, it uses Google's default style. Google's own logo and attribution are never covered. Outside maps, a white "Google Maps" label (Roboto 12 px, #5E5E5E on white) is used as required.

### Feedback
Banners are 14 px-radius tinted strips (amber by default, then info indigo, error red, success green) with a matching 22 to 24% border. Above the result cards, "No option meets every limit" is a single 13 px amber line in a 12 px-radius strip that names what still arrives on time, and the CO2 summary is a plain muted 12 px line with a small green leaf, not a box. They fade in from 4 px above over 240 ms and fade out on dismiss. Toasts float 96 px above the bottom on Pop. Skeletons are `fill-strong` blocks with a 1.5 s shimmer sweep, never spinners.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the CSS custom properties (`var(--accent)`, `var(--bg-surface)` and so on) rather than hex values, so Light and Dark both work.
- **Do** keep every tap target at 44 by 44 px or more. When the visual is smaller (chips, the clear dot, text links), extend the hit area with an invisible `::before`.
- **Do** squeeze pressables to `scale(0.98)` and use `--transition-smooth` (0.2s, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)) for hover, press and state changes.
- **Do** use `--push` for screen pushes, `--ease-out` for entrances and sheets, `--spring` only for small settling parts (knobs, thumbs, dialogs, chevrons), and cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1) for exits.
- **Do** show loading with shimmer skeletons shaped like the content that will arrive.
- **Do** give every coloured state a text label, and mark simulated values with the indigo "Simulated" tag.
- **Do** make large-area motion (pages, sheets, panels, toasts) out of `transform` and `opacity` only, and let `prefers-reduced-motion` or the in-app Reduce motion setting cut it to near-instant, with stagger delays dropped so every card appears at once.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use emoji as icons. Use the inline Lucide-style SVG set in `app.js`.
- **Don't** use spinning loaders.
- **Don't** introduce a second action colour. Platform Indigo is the only "act here" colour (see The One Signal Rule).
- **Don't** use red for anything that is not a disruption, a failure or a destructive action.
- **Don't** use `text-faint` for text a traveller must read. It measures below 4.5:1 on its backgrounds in both themes (see the appendix).
- **Don't** add borders thicker than 1 px, except for focus.
- **Don't** cover or restyle Google's map logo and attribution.

## Appendix: Code vs the original brief

This appendix is for the team. It records differences between [`docs/design/design-system.md`](docs/design/design-system.md) and the shipped code, and issues observed while extracting the tokens. The contrast issues were fixed on 2026-10-27 (see "Contrast fixes"); the others have not been fixed.

### Differences from the brief
1. **Themes.** The brief defines dark tokens only. The code adds a derived Light theme (`:root[data-theme="light"]`) and an Auto option that follows the system. Dark is the default.
2. **Token set.** The brief has 9 colour tokens. The code has about 40 per theme. The additions are void and pop surfaces, a strong border, a divider, faint text, accent text/soft/on-accent, success, error and warning families, teal, violet, fills, scrim, a translucent nav fill, nine mode colours and chart colours. The brief says "one accent"; the code keeps one action colour but adds status and mode hues.
3. **Font stack.** The brief's token list starts with Inter, but its example puts `-apple-system` first. The code puts Inter first, loads it from Google Fonts at 400 to 700, and adds `Segoe UI` and `Roboto` fallbacks.
4. **Radii.** The brief's example uses 16 px cards, 12 px interactive elements and 8 to 10 px skeletons and banners. The code uses 18 to 24 px cards, 14 px buttons, 12 px fields, 14 px banners and 10 to 14 px skeletons. The brief's `.interactive-element` (12 px) still exists in the code but is used only for the Plan a trip button, which overrides it to 20 px.
5. **Spacing.** The brief asks for "mathematical increments of 16px/24px" on a 4 px base. The code's gutter is 24 px (the brief's example body padding is 24 by 20 px), and it also uses 6, 10, 14 and 18 px values in many components.
6. **Transitions.** The brief asks for `transition: all 0.2s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` on every state change. The code uses that token for hover, press and state, and adds three more easings (`--push`, `--ease-out`, `--spring`), an exit curve, and longer durations of 300 to 600 ms for pages, sheets, panels and entrances.
7. **Hardware-accelerated properties only.** The brief asks that animations avoid layout properties. Large-area motion follows this, but the shared `all` transition also animates colours, borders and shadows. The map-picker pin also transitions `width`, `height`, `left`, `bottom` and `border-radius`. The brief's own `all` token conflicts with its rule.
8. **Focus states.** The brief's example uses a border change plus a 3 px glow. The code uses a global 2 px outline (offset 2 px) and adds a 5 px glow on buttons, chips and icon buttons. Fields follow the brief (border plus 3 px glow, no outline).
9. **Softer off-tones.** The brief asks to replace stark black and white with off-tones. The code still uses pure `#FFFFFF` for text on indigo, the checked switch knob, the live-directions maneuver icon, the demo dot, the QR card background and the offline area-box border.
10. **Libraries.** The brief mentions Framer Motion and Lucide or Heroicons. The code is plain CSS and JavaScript with no animation library, and its icons are a hand-drawn Lucide-style set rather than the Lucide package.
11. **Skeleton shimmer.** The brief's skeleton is a moving three-stop gradient over `bg-card` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`. The code uses a `fill-strong` block with a translating `--shimmer` sweep (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.055)` in Dark, 0.75 in Light), which matches the brief's "transform only" rule.
12. **Heading size.** The brief's example `h1` is 28 px. The code's screen titles are 30 px, and the home hero is 32 px.

### Contrast fixes (2026-10-27)
Measured with the WCAG 2.x contrast formula against the flat surface colours and the 10 to 15% tints laid over them, not on a device. Each fix is the smallest token or usage change that reaches the WCAG 2.2 AA 4.5:1 text threshold.
- **White on Platform Indigo in Dark** was 4.47:1 (#FFFFFF on #6366F1), and the Plan a trip sentence at 88% opacity was 3.84:1. Dark `accent` is now #575AF0: the same hue and saturation, with HSL lightness down from 66.7% to 64.1%. White on it is 5.09:1, and 4.53:1 in the 8% brighter hover state. The sentence no longer has reduced opacity. As a non-text colour (switch tracks, chip borders, the goal ring) it keeps at least 3.16:1 on every Dark surface. Light is unchanged at 6.29:1 (on #4F46E5). `accent-soft`, `accent-glow`, `border-focus` and the chart colour still mix from #6366F1.
- **`text-faint` on readable text** was 3.38 to 4.16:1 in Dark and 2.71 to 3.04:1 in Light. The token is unchanged, but placeholders, the suggestion-list footer (which carries the Google Maps attribution), the pending offline-download steps and the map-slot message now use `text-muted` (at least 6.27:1 in Dark and 5.20:1 in Light). `text-faint` remains only on decorative marks and disabled controls.
- **Light amber and green text on tinted chips** was 3.62 to 4.46:1 (for example `warning-text` #B45309 on its 15% tint over base, and "0 kg" on the Impact button at 4.21:1). Light `warning-text` is now #92400E and `success-text` is now #065F46, one step darker in the same scales. Over the 10 to 15% tints on base, surface and card they measure at least 5.11:1 (amber) and 5.50:1 (green). The tint tones (`warning`, `success`) are unchanged. Light `error-text` (#B91C1C) already passed at 4.54:1 or more on its tints, including on the Journey disrupted screen, so it is unchanged. Dark status text was already at 7.7:1 or more.

### Observed issues (not fixed)
- **Hard-coded colours outside the token set:** the dropped-pin red (#EA4335), the Lime and Neuron brand colours, the radar scale gradient, the Google attribution label, the map-marker colours and the dark map style in `app.js`, the logo gradient, and several inline `rgba(0, 0, 0, …)` shadows (switch knob, segmented thumb, demo pill, brand mark, desktop frame).
- **Light theme shadows:** the panel-edge, desktop-frame, knob and demo-pill shadows use black at the same alpha in both themes. Only the card and pop shadows have Light versions.
