# Audit: Reroute (`apps/plan-b-mvp`)

I checked the app against PRODUCT.md's WCAG 2.2 AA target and DESIGN.md. I changed no files. I ran the Impeccable detector on the source and on the running home page. I ran the app locally on port 8765 and tested it in Playwright at 390 px and 320 px wide, in Dark and Light. I also worked out contrast ratios for every token pair in both themes.

## Implementation Integrity Verdict

**Pass.** The code follows the system that DESIGN.md records: the surface ladder, one indigo signal, tinted status chips, tabular numbers and a text label on every coloured state. The detector found 116 items:

- **Font sizes (70) and radii (20):** mostly sizes the code really uses that DESIGN.md's front-matter scale leaves out. For example, 14 px appears 32 times in `app.css` and 17 px appears 10 times. This means the document is incomplete, not that the code drifted.
- **Hits I treat as false positives under "the brief wins":**
  - Inter as an overused font: the brief pins it.
  - Marquee: these are the documented shimmer skeletons.
  - Bounce easing: the documented `--spring`, used only on small parts.
  - The indigo glow on Plan a trip: a documented signature.
  - Kicker above heading: the documented eyebrow label.
  - Nested cards: the Surface Ladder rule allows inset boxes.
- **Not checked:** the repeating-stripes gradient.

## Executive Summary

- **Issues:** 0 P0, 5 P1, 3 P2, 4 P3.
- **Biggest problems:**
  1. Keyboard focus escapes the modal panels.
  2. Focus drops to `<body>` on every screen change.
  3. The two contrast failures already in DESIGN.md's appendix, which are worse than recorded.
  4. A new contrast failure: status chips in the Light theme.

## Findings by Severity

**[P1] Focus escapes modal panels** *(confirmed in the browser)*
- **Where:** `app.js:550` (menu), `app.js:2263` (Settings), `app.js:2707` (sheet), `app.js:2726` (popup) and `app.js:578` (alert dialog). Each sets `aria-modal="true"`, but nothing traps Tab or makes the page behind inert.
- **What I saw:** with the menu open, one Shift+Tab moved focus to "About / Data Sources", a link fully covered by the menu panel. 17 controls on the page behind the menu stayed focusable.
- **WCAG:** 2.4.3 Focus Order and 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), which is new in 2.2.
- **Fix:** while a panel is open, set `inert` on the current page and contain Tab inside the panel. The app already uses `inert` for the directions steps.
- **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Focus lost on every screen change** *(confirmed in the browser)*
- **Where:** `navigate()` at `app.js:2332` never moves focus.
- **What I saw:** after activating Plan a trip, focus was on `BODY`. The button that had focus is removed with the outgoing page. Nothing announces the new screen, and the page title stays "Reroute".
- **Impact:** keyboard and screen-reader users have to start again from the top on every screen.
- **WCAG:** 2.4.3. Screen-change announcements also relate to 4.1.3.
- **Fix:** when the transition ends, focus the new page's heading (with `tabindex="-1"`). Optionally update `document.title` as well.
- **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] White on Platform Indigo in Dark: 4.47:1** *(DESIGN.md appendix item; confirmed in the browser)*
- **Where:** `.btn-primary`, `.trip-btn` and every other surface with `--on-accent` text on `--accent` in Dark.
- **Large text does not apply.** The Plan a trip title is 19 px / 600. That is below the large-text threshold (24 px, or 18.66 px at 700), so it needs 4.5:1.
- **Worse than recorded:** the subline "Everyday routes, public transport first" has `opacity: 0.88` (`app.css:803`), which brings it down to **3.84:1**. The appendix does not list it.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3.
- **Fix:** darken the Dark `--accent` slightly. Light passes at 6.29:1.
- **Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] `text-faint` used for readable text** *(DESIGN.md appendix item)*
- **Contrast:** 3.38–4.16:1 in Dark and 2.71–3.04:1 in Light.
- **Readable text using it:**
  - `.ac-foot` (`app.css:361`), 11 px. This line carries the "Suggestions by Google Maps" attribution.
  - `.area-progress` pending download steps (`app.css:1187`), 13 px.
  - Input placeholders (`app.css:214`): 3.61:1 on Dark card, 3.01:1 on Light card. WCAG treats placeholder text as text.
  - `.map-skeleton` (`app.css:174`).
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3.
- **Fix:** switch these to `--text-muted`, which passes in both themes: at least 5.20:1 in Light and 6.27:1 in Dark.
- **Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Light theme: amber and green status text on tinted chips falls below 4.5:1** *(new; not in the appendix)*
- **Rendered on Home:** the Light Impact button "0 kg" measured **4.21:1** (`.impact-btn`, `app.css:808`).
- **Worked out from the tokens:**
  - `--warning-text` #B45309 on `--warning-soft`: 4.11:1 on surface, 4.28:1 on card, 3.87:1 as a banner on base.
  - `--success-text` on `--success-soft` over surface: 4.46:1.
- **Classes affected:**
  - `.rt.late` ("N min late"), `app.css:671`
  - `.fact.warn` and `.fact.good`, `app.css:436–437`
  - `.badge.tight`, `app.css:287`
  - `.feedback-banner` (default amber), `app.css:276`
  - `.wx-warn`, `app.css:655`
  - `.rt.on_time`, `app.css:670`
  - `.chip.live`, `app.css:267`
- **Impact:** "N min late" is exactly the information a traveller needs under time pressure.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3.
- **Fix:** darken the Light `--warning-text` (to about #92400E) and `--success-text` (to about #065F46). Dark passes everywhere, at 7.7:1 or more.
- **Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P2] Text-field edges are almost invisible**
- **Measurements:** the `--bg-card` field fill is 1.06:1 against `--bg-surface`, and `--border-subtle` is 1.16:1. The From and To rows have no box at all.
- **Impact:** labels and placeholders identify the fields, but the fields themselves are hard to see, especially outdoors.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast. Whether this fails is a judgement call.
- **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] Skipped heading levels**
- Home goes from `h1` to `h3` ("Translink right now", "Your impact this month", "Shared rides").
- The Plan a trip screen ("Where are you going?") has only an `h2` and no `h1`.
- **WCAG:** 1.3.1. Screen-reader users who navigate by heading lose structure.
- **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] `transition: all` and layout animation**
- `--transition-smooth: all …` (`app.css:20`) is used on most components, so every property animates, including shadows and borders.
- The map-picker pin animates `width`, `height`, `left`, `bottom` and `border-radius` (appendix item 7).
- There is also an 18 px backdrop blur on both the nav bar and the sticky footer.
- I did not measure performance, so the effect on frame rate is unknown.
- **Command:** `/impeccable optimize`

**[P3] Hero pin overlaps the headline**
- At 390 px, in both themes, the white location pin in the hero art sits on the "k." of "break." (seen in screenshots).
- **Command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P3] Hard-coded colours and Light shadows** *(appendix items; confirmed by the detector)*
- `#000` at `app.css:319`.
- `rgba(0,0,0,…)` at `app.css:247`, `689`, `1020–1024` and `1174–1175`.
- Brand colours for the hire services at `app.css:1142–1145`.
- The radar gradient at `app.css:1115`.
- **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

**[P3] DESIGN.md scales are incomplete**
- The front-matter type scale lacks 14, 17, 24 and 26 px and others that the code uses widely.
- The radius scale lacks 3, 4, 8, 9, 11 and 22 px.
- Until this is fixed, the detector will keep reporting about 90 false drift hits.
- **Command:** `/impeccable document`

**[P3] Segmented control has no accessible name**
- Its `role="group"` has no `aria-label` (`app.js:294`). The buttons inside it are fine.

## Patterns and Systemic Issues

- **Focus management is missing.** Focus moves when a panel opens and returns when it closes, but nothing traps it while the panel is open and nothing places it on a new screen. One shared fix covers all five panels and `navigate()`.
- **Light-theme contrast was not checked against tinted backgrounds.** The `-text` tokens pass on plain surfaces but fail once the 10% tints are laid under them.

## Positive Findings

- Every tap target I measured is 44 px or larger. Small visuals get invisible hit-area extensions, such as `.fine a::before`.
- Reflow at 320 px causes no horizontal scroll.
- The viewport does not block zoom.
- Each colour state is paired with a text label.
- The autocomplete is a correct ARIA combobox: listbox, `aria-activedescendant` and `aria-expanded`.
- Toasts and live updates use `role="status"`, the alert dialog is labelled and described, and the offline map supports the keyboard (arrow keys and plus/minus).
- Reduced motion works both from the system setting and from the in-app setting, and it removes the looping shimmer and ping animations.
- Dark text contrast is excellent everywhere except on indigo and with `text-faint`.

## Recommended Actions

1. **[P1] `/impeccable harden`:** contain focus and set the background inert for the menu, Settings, sheets, popups and the dialog. Focus the new heading in `navigate()`. Fix the heading levels.
2. **[P1] `/impeccable colorize`:** darken the Dark `--accent` and drop the subline opacity. Replace `text-faint` on readable text. Darken the Light `--warning-text` and `--success-text`.
3. **[P2] `/impeccable optimize`:** replace `transition: all` with named properties and move the picker pin's animation to transforms.
4. **[P3] `/impeccable document`:** add the missing type sizes and radii to DESIGN.md, and record the new Light contrast finding in its appendix.
5. **[P3] `/impeccable polish`:** field edges, the hero overlap, and the hard-coded colours and shadows. Run this last.

You can ask me to run these one at a time, all at once, or in any order you prefer. Re-run `/impeccable audit` after fixes to see your score improve.
